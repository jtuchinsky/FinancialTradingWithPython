# 2. The concept of trading

Summary of the DataCamp exercise
[*The concept of trading*](https://campus.datacamp.com/courses/financial-trading-in-python/trading-basics-1?ex=2).

**Exercise type:** Multiple choice (theory — no coding).

## Core idea

Financial trading is the **buying and selling of financial assets with the goal of making a
profit**. It involves transactions in asset markets, and profit generation is the primary objective.

## The question

You are asked to identify **which statement about trading is incorrect** — a quick check on the
concepts from the previous video (long vs. short positions, trading vs. investing, and technical
analysis). See [lesson 1](../01-what-is-financial-trading/README.md) for those definitions.
